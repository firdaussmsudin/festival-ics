Film Festival Calendar

A subscribable calendar of film festival dates, scraped weekly and kept up to date automatically.

Subscribe once and new festivals, date changes, or cancellations show up in your calendar app automatically — no re-downloading, no manual updates.

Subscribe to the calendar

If the button above doesn't work in your calendar app, copy this URL and add it manually (in Google Calendar: Other calendars → + → From URL):

https://firdaussmsudin.github.io/festival-ics/festivals.ics

Data is scraped weekly and reviewed before publishing. Found a wrong date or a missing festival? Use the feedback button in the corner to report it.
